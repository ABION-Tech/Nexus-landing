How it runs

Your team,
run to a rhythm.

NEXUS doesn't wait to be checked. It works the day on schedule — billing, sweeps, digests, reports — on West Africa Time.

  1. 07:00

    It checks billing.

    Plans, renewals and any lapsed payment are settled before the first person logs in.

  2. 08:00

    It flags what's late.

    Every overdue task in every company is marked, so nobody has to go looking.

  3. 08:05

    The digest goes out.

    The morning digest: what's open, what's due, what's stuck, for each person and each company.

  4. 16:00

    It calls close.

    A reminder to wrap up the day's tasks while there is still time to finish them.

  5. 18:05

    Reports land.

    A nudge to everyone who hasn't filed their end-of-day report. Leaders read them in one place.

Who is NEXUS for?+
Any team or company that runs on tasks, deadlines and daily reports — from a five-person startup to a group with several companies. It's built for the people who keep the work moving: founders, operations leads, chiefs of staff, executive assistants and team leads.
How do people sign in?+
With their email and a password they choose themselves, through a one-time link we email them. Google sign-in is coming soon.
How does someone join?+
An admin invites them by email and picks their role. They get a link, choose a password, and land in their company.
Can one company see another's work?+
No. Each company's data is separated inside the database itself, not just hidden in the screens. Only the group, and NEXUS's own administrators, see across.
What roles are there?+
Leader, team member and intern in each company, with extra permissions granted one at a time. Group and company admins manage their own people.
Does it work on a bad connection?+
Yes. Changes made offline are queued on the device and sent in order when the signal returns, and drafts are saved as you type.
Is there a phone app?+
NEXUS installs from the browser on any phone, and there is an Android app.
Which AI does it use?+
Yours. Each company connects its own AI provider and key, and NEXUS records what each feature uses so there are no surprises.
What are end-of-day reports?+
A short note each person files at close: what got done, what's blocked, what's next. NEXUS reminds whoever hasn't filed by 18:05.
How do we start?+
Request access with your company's name and how many people you have (and, if you run several companies, how many). We set it up and invite your first admins.
