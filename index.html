HOW A DAY RUNS

It works the day so you don't chase it.

Five things happen on their own, every working day, on West Africa Time. Nobody has to remember to check.

08:05 Now in AbujaNext: the morning digest
  1. 07:00

    Billing is settled.

    Renewals and seat counts are checked before anyone logs in, so nobody is locked out at 9.

  2. 08:00

    Late work is flagged.

    Every task past its date is marked overdue, across every team. Nobody has to go looking.

  3. 08:05

    Everyone gets their digest.

    What's open, what's due today and what's stuck, written for each person and sent to their phone and inbox.

  4. 16:00

    The afternoon close.

    A reminder to wrap up today's tasks while there's still time to finish them, not after.

    Still open today 16:00 reminder
    • November payroll sign-offdue 17:00
    • Partner MoU: FCT logisticsdue today
  5. 18:05

    Reports come in.

    Each person files three lines: done, blocked, tomorrow. Whoever hasn't filed gets a nudge. Leaders read them all in one place.

QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

Who is NEXUS for?

Any team or company that runs on tasks, deadlines and daily reports, from a five-person startup to a group with several companies. It's built for the people who keep work moving: founders, operations leads, chiefs of staff, executive assistants and team leads.

How is it different from a to-do app?

A to-do app waits for you to open it. NEXUS runs the day on a schedule: it flags late work at 08:00, sends each person their digest at 08:05, calls the close at 16:00 and collects end-of-day reports at 18:05. Leaders see the whole team without asking.

How do people sign in?

With their email and a password they choose themselves, set through a one-time link we email them. Google sign-in is coming soon.

How does someone join?

An admin invites them by email and picks their role. They get a link, choose a password, and land in the right company.

Does it work on a bad connection?

Yes. Changes made offline are queued on the device and sent in order when the signal returns, and drafts save as you type.

Which AI does it use?

Yours. Each company connects its own AI provider and key, and NEXUS records what each feature uses, so there are no surprise bills.

Can one company see another's work?

No. Each company's records are separated inside the database itself. Only a group you set up, and NEXUS's own administrators, can see across companies.

How do we start?

Request access with your company's name and how many people you have. We set it up with you and invite your first admins. Teams of up to five can stay on the Free plan.
