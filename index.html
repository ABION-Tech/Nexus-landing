Questions, answered

Ayo
Which company is falling behind this week?
AI analyst
ABION Industries. 3 of its 4 overdue tasks sit with facilities, and 2 EOD reports were missed on Thursday. The lease renewal was closed today.

Who is NEXUS for? Groups of companies and the operations people who hold them together: founders, chiefs of staff, executive assistants and team leads who need every company's work in one place.

How do people sign in? With their email and a password they choose themselves, through a one-time link we email them. Google sign-in is available too.

How does someone join? An admin invites them by email and picks their role. They get a link, choose a password, and land in their company.

Can one company see another's work? No. Each company's data is separated inside the database itself, not just hidden in the screens. Only the group, and NEXUS's own administrators, see across.

What roles are there? Leader, team member and intern in each company, with extra permissions granted one at a time. Group and company admins manage their own people.

Does it work on a bad connection? Yes. Changes made offline are queued on the device and sent in order when the signal returns, and drafts are saved as you type.

Is there a phone app? NEXUS installs from the browser on any phone, and there is an Android app.

Which AI does it use? Yours. Each company connects its own AI provider and key, and NEXUS records what each feature uses so there are no surprises.

What are end-of-day reports? A short note each person files at close: what got done, what's blocked, what's next. NEXUS reminds whoever hasn't filed by 18:05.

How do we start? Request access with your group's name and size. We set up the group and its companies and invite your first admins.
